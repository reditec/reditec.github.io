SparkIV

A free file browser, viewer and editor for Grand Theft Auto IV and Episodes from Liberty City on PC, with a cutscene viewer that renders like the game. New: experimental, read-only support for Red Dead Redemption 2 (PC) and Grand Theft Auto VI (Xbox Series).

What it does

Open the game's archives, look inside every file and change them safely. SparkIV reads the files of your own copy of the game; it contains no game data and no keys.

Archives

Browse the IMG and RPF archives of GTA IV, The Lost and Damned and The Ballad of Gay Tony.

Red Dead Redemption 2 .rpf

Experimental, read-only: browse the game's RPF8 archives with their file names. A one-time assistant reads the keys from the running game.

Grand Theft Auto VI .rpf .ttpf

Experimental, read-only: browse the archives of the Xbox Series version and its texture pools; textures and audio open in the same viewers as GTA IV's. The cipher is read from the game's own executable.

Cutscenes .cut

Plays the cutscenes with cast, camera, lights, sound and location, lit and shaded the way the game does it.

Models .wdr .wdd .wft

Models in 3D, with their textures and shaders.

Textures .wtd .ztd .dds

View and export the textures of a dictionary, GTA VI's texture pools and DDS files.

Animations .wad

Play the animations of a dictionary.

Audio .ivaud .awc

Listen to sound banks, including multichannel tracks and GTA VI's radio stations, and export them.

Scripts .sco

Decompiled or disassembled mission scripts.

Collision .wbn .wbd

See the collision of the map.

Editing

Replace and import files of GTA IV and the episodes in an edit mode. The original of every file is backed up before SparkIV first writes to it.

Get started

A legally owned copy of a supported game is required: GTA IV or Episodes from Liberty City (PC), Red Dead Redemption 2 (PC) or Grand Theft Auto VI (Xbox Series). SparkIV may only be used for offline modding.

Download

Get reditec.SparkIV-win-Setup.exe from the releases.

Install

Run it. As the app isn't signed yet, Windows may warn you: choose "More info", then "Run anyway".

Open your game

SparkIV finds the game folder or asks for it. New versions install with one click and show what's new; the channel is set in Settings → Updates.

What's new in 0.9.0 Alpha 7

Released on November 25, 2026. SparkIV opens Red Dead Redemption 2 and Grand Theft Auto VI (experimental, read-only), and the cutscenes come closer to the game.

  • Red Dead Redemption 2 (PC): browse the RPF8 archives with their file names; a one-time assistant reads the keys from the running game.
  • Grand Theft Auto VI (Xbox Series): browse the RPF9 archives, also archives inside archives; encrypted and compressed files open as their real content, and file names are recovered from the game's own strings.
  • GTA VI's textures (.ztd), texture pools (.ttpf) and audio (.awc, also the radio stations) open in the same texture and audio viewers as GTA IV's.
  • One "Choose Game" button opens the game selector; edit mode stays off for RDR2 and GTA VI.
  • Cutscenes: street lamps and traffic lights glow, the map's smoke and steam show, vehicle headlamps, lamp shadows, characters, sky, water and fog are closer to the game.
  • New settings "Texture filtering" and "Smooth edges" for the 3D views.

Where it's heading

Version 1.0 will be the first that fully supports GTA IV on PC:

  • every file of the game opens;
  • all cutscenes look as they do in the game;
  • export, edit and import, compatible with OpenIV's openFormats.

Known differences from the game are tracked in the issues.
